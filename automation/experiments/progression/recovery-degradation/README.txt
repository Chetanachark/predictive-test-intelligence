Recovery degradation: semantic checkout link changes increasingly require the
existing HealingEngine to recover and verify navigation. A transient removal
requires an actual retry; final indistinguishable links force recovery refusal.
Healing evidence is emitted by the real engine and no production knowledge base
is trained on these experiments. This overlaps locator degradation by design;
claims of generalization across independent fault mechanisms are not justified.
